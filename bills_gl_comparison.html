Data Structure Summary

Source Key Columns Purpose
Bills bill_inv, container, sipl_inv, amount, invoice_dt Vendor invoices entered by Invoice Team
GL 1313 invoice, description, date, debit/credit Direct freight costs confirmed by Accounting
GL 1275 invoice, description, date, debit/credit Indirect freight costs confirmed by Accounting

What I Need To Understand

    • Is there a bill_status column?
    • Or is it just that GL doesn't have it yet?
  2. Missing Invoice: When there's no bill AND no GL entry, what should I look for?
    • Is it simply: Container/SIPL exists but no bill_inv row?
    • Or is there a placeholder like "PENDING" that means awaiting vendor?
  3. Complete Invoice: When GL has the invoice, it's Complete (confirmed)?
